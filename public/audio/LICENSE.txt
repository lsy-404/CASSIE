The files in this directory (*.opus) are ADAPTATIONS of that work, not the
original recordings. Changes made:

  - audio clips were extracted from the installed game's Unity asset archives;
  - each clip was decoded and re-encoded to mono, 48 kHz, 48 kbit/s VBR Ogg Opus;
  - cassie-start and cassie-end are excerpts cut from a longer background clip
    using time windows derived from the game's data.

Per-file provenance (source archive, object ID, hashes) is recorded in
data/source-manifest.json and public/bank.json in the source repository.
